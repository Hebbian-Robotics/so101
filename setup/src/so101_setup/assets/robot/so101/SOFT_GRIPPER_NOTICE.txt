Source CAD and generation tooling are in models/soft-gripper-camera in the
SO-101 source repository. The upstream source has no declared license; these
derived meshes are not covered by the stock model's Apache-2.0 license or this
package's MIT software license. Redistribution terms remain unspecified.

The camera envelope is 38 x 38 mm across the board and reserves 16.05 mm in
front of its mounting plane plus 6.2 mm behind for the rear connector. Flexible
cable geometry is excluded. This is not detailed camera CAD. Its board frame
has +Z toward the scene and +Y toward the connector
edge. It is not a calibrated optical frame. The grasp frame is the midpoint
between the inward surfaces of the distal 1 mm of both unloaded fingers at
model gripper angle zero. Verify alignment and endpoints on the installed arm.
